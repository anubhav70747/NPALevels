## Material new finding

[Sun–Wang–Yu, arXiv:2610.34364v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.34364v1), submitted **25 October 2026**, supplies a new full-set characterization. Primary submission history lists v1 only; a third-party “updated October 27” label is not evidence of a new arXiv version.

Theorem 1 states that every quantum behavior is a mixture of **one pure real two-qubit behavior from S** and a local behavior. Section I explicitly says S strictly contains the extreme set E. Thus the theorem statement is broader in its allowed component than the abstract's wording “extreme quantum correlation.” Theorem 6 asserts uniqueness of the behavior maximizing every Bell functional with strict quantum advantage. Theorems 2–3 treat maximal faces containing a nonlocal exposed point; positivity faces require separate treatment. Inspected: Sections I–III; Appendix B's determinant/overlap argument and face consequences; Appendix C's minimum-weight decomposition route; Appendix E's exact semialgebraic feasibility formulation and benchmark limitations. This was statement/argument inspection, not an independent proof validation.

No universal standard-word SOS or NPA admission theorem appears. The new membership reduction therefore does **not** classify which supporting Bell functionals first become exact at 1+AB or another finite standard NPA level.

## Search and remaining link

Live searches combined full/minimal quantum geometry, extremality, (2,2,2), standard NPA, 1+AB, tilted/doubly tilted CHSH, exactness, SOS, 2025 and 2026. Additional searches excluded the known July/September IDs and used author/title searches. Only primary arXiv, journal and author outputs support the table; third-party aggregators were discovery leads. The search found the October 25 characterization and the 2026 ncKKT version, and checked current primary version histories for the principal existing leads. It did not find a theorem classifying all full-(2,2,2) Bell objectives by first exact standard NPA level.

The missing implication is concrete: an explicit geometry or finite commutative/qubit optimization description must be lifted to an identity in the **universal measurement algebra**, with square factors supported on the specified standard word list and with exact PSD coefficients. Uniqueness of a physical maximizer, a Jordan reduction, finite-dimensional membership decidability, enhanced KKT constraints, or a conditioned certificate is not that lift. Exact value support and exact recovery of the optimal behavior face are also separate requirements.

The bounded literature conclusion is therefore: the user's belief about a full geometric characterization is correct, and an additional very recent membership characterization was missed by the initial inventory. Neither inspected theorem closes the standard 1+AB/all-finite-level admission classification.
